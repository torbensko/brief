# Brief 1.2.0

- Optional **Tidy wording** removes obvious stutters and empty filler and makes small grammatical fixes after recognition.
- A separate local model checks edits for changes in meaning and emphasis. Questionable or failed edits fall back to the saved original.
- See highlighted additions and struck-through removals in the floating panel and Recent. Switch to clean text or copy the original.
- Cleanup is off by default. Download its model from Settings → General to try it. Messages remain on your Mac.

Automatic insertion, clipboard recovery, and existing recognition remain available with cleanup off. Both text versions follow your short recovery retention settings.
